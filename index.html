Privacy Policy for Health Tracker

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

Medical Disclaimer: Health Tracker is designed strictly for general health, wellness, and self-tracking purposes. It is not a medical device, and the information presented (including BMI estimates, fasting timers, and exercise logs) is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition or provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making health-related decisions.

1. Data Collection and Storage

Health Tracker is built with your privacy as a foundational principle:

  • All data stays on your device: All health metrics you log (water intake, sleep hours, exercise duration, body weight, mood, fasting timer progress, and height) are stored locally in your device's isolated storage (AsyncStorage).
  • Zero data collection: We do not operate user accounts, cloud databases, analytics servers, or tracking pixels. We do not collect, read, or process your personal information.
  • Zero data sharing: Because no data leaves your device, none of your personal or health information is ever transmitted, shared, sold, or disclosed to third parties or service providers.

2. App Permissions

Health Tracker requests only the bare minimum system access required for core offline utilities:

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATION): Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback when the exercise timer countdown finishes. No personal data is accessed or stored.
  • No location, camera, microphone, or storage permissions are requested or used.

3. Advertisements and Analytics

Health Tracker does not contain third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or telemetry analytics.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored exclusively on your device, you maintain 100% control over your data at all times:

  • You can delete all your logged data instantly at any time from within the app by navigating to the Goals tab and tapping "Clear all data".
  • Uninstalling the app from your device will permanently erase all stored application data.

5. Children's Privacy

Health Tracker is intended for general audiences and individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
